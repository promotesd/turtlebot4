# Code of Conduct

We commit to a respectful, harassment-free community for people of every background,
identity, experience level, and ability. Be constructive, assume good intent while
addressing impact, credit others' work, and keep technical disagreement focused on
evidence and project goals.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention, and publication of private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may
edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation
when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to `1412822254@qq.com`. Reports will be reviewed
promptly and kept confidential as far as practical. Enforcement decisions should be
proportionate, documented privately, and avoid conflicts of interest.
